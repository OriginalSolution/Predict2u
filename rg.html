If you need help in Ghana now

Both lines below are free to call from Ghana. You do not need to be in crisis — “I’m betting more than I meant to” is reason enough.

Gaming Commission of Ghana 0800 454 545 Also 0274 698 935 · Self-exclusion requests & complaints about licensed operators · gamingcommission.gov.gh Mental Health Authority 0800 678 678 National mental health & crisis line — also supports gambling-related distress Gambling Therapy (international) Free online chat & forums · gamblingtherapy.org

Self-exclusion

  • With the operator — every Ghana-licensed book should offer deposit limits and self-exclusion in your account. Use them while you are calm.
  • Through the Commission — call 0800 454 545 if you need a multi-operator exclusion or an operator is not honouring one.

Four habits that help

  1. Set a limit first — only use money you can afford to lose. Never stake rent, food, school fees or bills.
  2. Don’t chase losses — a loss does not make the next pick more likely to win.
  3. Read the record honestly — wins and losses both count. Short streaks are not proof.
  4. Take a break — if it stops feeling controlled, step away and call a help line above.
